RELAY is a school assistance app for students who need help quickly, including those who cannot speak comfortably. The opening screen puts floor selection before the help categories, so students can share a useful location without knowing a room number. They can review the request, contact a teacher or send a message to the school inbox. The prototype demonstrates automatic escalation to the office when a request has no acknowledgement after 30 seconds. A teacher response and an adult arriving are shown as separate steps. Large controls, clear labels and a text-only option guide the phone experience. The school crest and notebook doodles give it a familiar identity, while light travelling through the background waves adds character. Requests and notifications are simulated for this design submission.
