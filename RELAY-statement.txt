I designed RELAY for students at DPS Modern Indian School who need help quickly, especially when they are frightened or cannot speak. The journey starts with choosing a help type, then a familiar campus location. A clear review screen helps catch mistakes before sending. Students can reach a teacher, send a ping or continue by text, and see when Mrs. Aditi has accepted responsibility. The request ends only after the student confirms that an adult is with them. I used large controls, plain language and distinct symbols so students do not have to rely on colour alone. Soft waves and floating 3D symbols give RELAY its identity. Light and dark themes keep the interface comfortable to read.
