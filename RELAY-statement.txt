RELAY is a school assistance app for students who need help quickly or cannot speak comfortably. New requests start with Medical help selected, which students can change with one tap. Floors and A, B and C Block are shown as large buttons, with the block optional. Students can review their details, contact a teacher or message the school inbox. The prototype demonstrates office escalation after 30 seconds without acknowledgement. A teacher replying and an adult arriving are treated as different steps, so the request stays open until handover. Readable text, clear navigation and quick replies guide the phone experience. School notebook drawings and light travelling along the waves give it a familiar identity. Requests and notifications are simulated for this submission.
