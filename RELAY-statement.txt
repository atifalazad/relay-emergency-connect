Najda helps students request school assistance quickly, including when speaking is difficult. Help choices appear straight away, with a prominent location shortcut. Four separate phone screens guide the main journey: help type, location, review and response. Large floor buttons and an optional block choice make location simple. Large icons and visible Back and Next controls keep each step clear without page scrolling. A countdown shows when an unanswered request will escalate. The office follow-up screen then offers a call, staff messages and an arrival check. Choosing “No, I still need help” keeps the request open, preserves the location and adds follow-up again. Only an explicit adult handover confirmation closes it. School notebook drawings and light moving through the waves give Najda its identity. Requests and notifications are simulated for this design submission.
